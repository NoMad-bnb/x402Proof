Independent verification · x402 ecosystem

The chain is the source.

402Proof is an independent audit and reputation layer for x402 facilitators. Facilitator claims are checked against blockchain evidence. Verdicts are produced through GenLayer consensus, not a single trusted backend.

What 402Proof is not: a facilitator, a wallet, or a settlement rail. It observes settlement. It does not execute it.

Why 402Proof exists

The facilitator is trusted. Nobody checks.

In the x402 protocol, a facilitator receives a signed payment authorization, settles on chain, and tells the seller whether it succeeded or failed. That answer is not verified by anyone independent.

Did it settle?

When a facilitator claims success or failure, there is no independent way to confirm the claim against the chain.

Silent discrimination

A facilitator could serve some clients and ignore others. Without a public evidence layer, that behaviour stays invisible.

Declaration vs behaviour

What a facilitator publishes about networks and addresses may not match what it actually does on chain.

402Proof answers these questions with chain evidence and independent consensus. It does not replace the facilitator. It audits it.

Evidence & verification

Verification records

402Proof does not assert trust with a single score. It publishes the evidence and the verdict family that follows from it. Six families stay separate. They are never merged or averaged.

CONFIRMED Claim and chain match
CONTRADICTED Claim disagrees with chain
SETTLED_ Settled without a captured announcement
REJECTED_ Claim refused; suffix states why
PENDING_ No final verdict yet
UNDETERMINED_ Problem on our side; never counts against the operator

Absence is not a verdict. Pending stays pending until evidence is final. Undetermined is never counted against the operator.

Architecture

How 402Proof works

Observation, independent verification, then public record. No step relies on trusting a single server’s word about what happened on chain.

Facilitator

Receives a signed payment authorization and settles on chain, or claims it did.

x402 claim

A settlement response or a discovered candidate transaction becomes a claim to verify.

Chain evidence

Receipt, Transfer events, and EIP-3009 authorization data are read from the chain.

GenLayer verification

Independent validators each fetch the same evidence. Results must match byte-for-byte.

Evidence record

A durable verdict is written only when consensus holds. No match, no write.

Public reputation

Registry and verification data are published. Counts keep their denominators. No single score.

Independent verification

Why GenLayer

A normal backend is one machine you are asked to trust. That would recreate the same trust problem x402 already has, under a different name.

GenLayer makes multiple independent validators each fetch the receipt themselves, then compare results with strict_eq. If the bytes differ, nothing is written. Independence is measured, not assumed.

  • What is checked: settlement claims against chain evidence (receipts, transfers, EIP-3009 authorization).
  • Judgment is deterministic. Language models are not part of settlement verdicts.
  • 402Proof does not execute payments, hold funds, or send settlement transactions.

Design principle

The chain is the source. Everything else is a cache.

Independent reads must agree under gl.eq_principle.strict_eq. The public record is derived from verified evidence, not from a private indexer’s opinion alone.
